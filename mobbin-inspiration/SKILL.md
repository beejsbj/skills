# Mobbin as a reference source

[Mobbin](https://mobbin.com) is a searchable library of screenshots and flows from shipped mobile and web apps (the site advertises 600k+). Burooj uses it as the first stop for UI/UX inspiration.

## When

Before designing or critiquing a screen, flow, or component: onboarding, paywall, empty state, settings, checkout, navigation, forms, tables, search, notifications. Skip it for pure visual identity work (brand marks, type, colour), where galleries suited to that medium do the job.

## How

1. Search by the screen type, UI element, or flow, in the signed-in browser. If Mobbin asks for a sign-in or shows a paywall, stop and ask Burooj; never create an account or enter credentials.
2. Look at three to five comparable shipped examples. Note what most of them converge on, and which one diverges on purpose.
3. Bring back a short set: app, screen, link, and the one pattern worth borrowing or avoiding. Take the pattern, not the pixels.
4. Do not bulk-download or scrape screenshots. Link to them.

The project's own design language decides the outcome; Mobbin feeds the options. With the Design Lab, this belongs in the reference-gathering step of Design Exploration.
