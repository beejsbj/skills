# Phase 4 — Whole sentences interleaved

## Weave ratio
Roughly **half** target language, at the sentence level. You switch language between sentences, sometimes mid-paragraph. The base language now appears where precision or difficulty demands it, not as the default floor. The user is expected to reply at least partly in the target language.

## What to introduce
- **Past / completed action**, and the language's aspect or tense contrasts, by saturation rather than rule. (Spanish: preterite `fue`, `dijo`, `hice` for completed events; imperfect `era`, `había`, `pensaba` seeping in by contrast.)
- **Irrealis / mood / politeness** in its most common live frames, by exposure only — the user feels the mood before they can name it. (Spanish subjunctive: `quiero que...`, `espero que...`, `aunque...`, `para que...`.)
- **Idiom and register** — the connective texture that makes it stop sounding like a textbook. (Spanish: `o sea`, `de hecho`, `la verdad`, `en plan`.)

## Handling the user's production
Expect target-language replies and engage with their *content*, not their correctness. Recast errors invisibly by using the right form in your reply. Push gently toward expressing a whole thought in the target language before switching out. If they retreat to the base language for something hard, follow them there without comment, then drift back once it's resolved.

## Promote to Phase 5 when
They sustain multi-sentence target language comfortably, reach for the base language mainly for genuinely hard thinking (not as a default escape), and past-tense + common mood frames appear in their own production. Hold if output is still mostly simplest-tense and effortful.

## Worked examples (Spanish, illustrative)
> La verdad, creo que ya entiendes la reificación mejor que mucha gente. O sea, no es solo una idea — es una manera de ver. When you said process freezes into thing, that's exactly Nāgārjuna's point.

> Ayer pensaba en lo que dijiste sobre el Cosmere. ¿Qué pasaría si el Reino Cognitivo es literalmente esto — ideas que se vuelven cosas? Tell me where that breaks.
