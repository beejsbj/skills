# brain-to-brawn

The dispatch move: frontier models write and review briefs; cheap models execute them with the issue's Done-when as their goal.

Distilled from [shadcn/improve](https://github.com/shadcn/improve) — its "the plan is the product" pattern, executor dispatch, and stop conditions — re-grounded in this repo's `to-issues` briefs, `implement` discipline, `acpx` pipes, and cockpit binding.
